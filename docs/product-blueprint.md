# AutoAxis DMS case-study blueprint

## Product thesis

AutoAxis is an Automotive Integration Hub, not a collection of department applications. Customer 360° and Vehicle 360° are the shared records. Every lead, test drive, deal, service job, part, policy, communication, valuation, and ownership event contributes to the same relationship and asset timelines.

## Benchmark baseline

Titan positions its platform around integrated showroom, service, administration, parts, CRM, reporting, APIs, appraisal, barcoding, analytics, and OEM data exchange. Pentana presents integrated DMS, CRM, service automation, accounting, parts, business intelligence, data warehousing, and mobile workflows. AutoAxis must demonstrate these expected capabilities while making cross-department workflows more visible and easier to understand.

## Differentiating demonstration journeys

1. Search a mobile number and reveal the customer's household, companies, vehicles, enquiries, purchases, service, parts, finance, insurance, complaints, communications, profitability, and next-best actions.
2. Search a VIN and reveal acquisition, owners, condition, inspections, workshop work, parts, warranty, insurance, valuation, inventory location, aging, and resale margin.
3. Convert an enquiry into test drive, appraisal, quote, KYC, finance, insurance, accessories, allocation, registration, delivery, and follow-up without re-entering customer or vehicle data.
4. Convert a service booking into diagnosis, digital approval, technician scheduling, parts reservation, warranty/insurance decision, invoicing, payment, and retention activity.
5. Roll every operational event into branch and group profitability, customer lifetime value, vehicle margin, workforce productivity, and OEM reporting.

## Dashboard scope

- Executive overview: group health, revenue, margin, CX, risks, and exceptions.
- Customer 360°: relationship graph, timeline, profitability, communication, consent, complaints, and opportunities.
- Vehicle 360°: VIN lifecycle, ownership, service, condition, warranty, valuation, and resale.
- Sales: leads, pipeline, test drives, quotes, bookings, delivery, and follow-up.
- Service: bookings, bays, repair orders, technicians, approvals, warranty, and CSI.
- Parts: catalog, availability, reservations, replenishment, warehouse, suppliers, and margin.
- Finance and insurance: applications, lender/policy comparisons, compliance, renewals, claims, and commission.
- Marketing: audiences, journeys, campaigns, attribution, consent, retention, and ROI.
- Used vehicles: acquisition, inspection, appraisal, refurbishment, pricing, publishing, aging, and margin.
- Inventory: VIN stock, location, allocation, incoming vehicles, transfers, PDI, aging, and valuation.
- Branch: targets, department performance, workforce, operational risks, and local profitability.
- Group: consolidated performance, comparisons, forecasting, OEM scorecards, and drill-down analytics.

## Case-study evidence policy

All public claims must be approved and traceable. Until customer evidence is supplied, screenshots and outcomes must be labelled as demonstration data, illustrative workflow, target outcome, or product concept. Invented customer quotes and unsupported performance statistics must not be published as facts.
